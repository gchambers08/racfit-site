The Party

Celebrate your
young athlete.

Our sports environment lets kids jump into tennis, pickleball, athletic challenges, digital sports games, ping pong and more - all in one unforgettable celebration.

Your group gets a fun, active, health-forward party experience designed to keep everyone smiling, moving and making memories. Whether they're seasoned athletes or trying something new, our team keeps it safe, engaging and exciting for all ages.

A dedicated host

A birthday ambassador keeps the action flowing so you get to actually enjoy the party.

Real coaching

An hour of tennis or pickleball instruction from a RacFit professional, pitched to the age group.

Rain or shine

Indoor tennis and covered pickleball courts mean the party goes ahead whatever the weather does.

Digital Sports room

Access to the MultiBall interactive wall, with customizable game modes for the group.

Games beyond the court

Ping pong and corn hole for everyone between sessions.

Courtside Cantina

Reserved tables for the parents, with food and drink available on site.
