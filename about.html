Mission

To positively impact the lives of children and adults by providing innovative racket-based training, fitness, entertainment and healthy lifestyle programs that accomplish the health and fitness needs of families in our surrounding communities.

Vision

To be the premier health and fitness choice for our surrounding communities - with innovative, rain-or-shine programming around tennis, pickleball and fitness.

Our Journey

Driven
by purpose.

RacFit began as a conversation between friends - local tennis and pickleball enthusiasts who shared a dream to create something lasting for the communities we call home. Five years ago, that dream took root: to build a space where fitness, sport and community would come together in a way that felt local, inclusive and deeply personal.

While each of us brings decades of experience in tennis, pickleball, fitness and hospitality, we're not traditional developers. We've built this from the ground up with determination, teamwork and the wisdom of those who have guided us along the way.

We've poured ourselves into this project not just because we love the game, but because tennis and pickleball have changed our lives. That's why RacFit is more than a facility - it's a reflection of the values we believe in: health, hard work, connection and joy.

We're incredibly grateful for the community of Buda, who welcomed us with open arms - and deeply thankful to our nearly 40 investors, passionate advocates who believed in this vision early on and helped turn it into something real. Whether you're stepping onto a court for the first time, rediscovering your love for movement, or simply looking for community, RacFit is here to serve, inspire and grow with you.
The Facility

What's
inside.

22,000 sq ft indoor

Climate-controlled space with locker rooms - so the schedule runs rain, shine or August.

8 tennis courts

Two of them indoor, six outdoor. Full junior and adult programming across every level.

8 pickleball courts

Regulation courts, four of them covered by a steel shade structure.

2 fitness rooms

Two dedicated studios running a full weekly class schedule, including Les Mills.

Full bar

Courtside Cantina, with indoor and outdoor seating and a view of the action.

Digital Sports room

The MultiBall interactive wall - real balls, real movement, digital games.

On-site ActiveCare

Credentialed daycare for ages 3+, so you can train while the kids do their thing.

Community social spaces

Large indoor lounges and an expansive outdoor greenspace - plus three food trucks. And more.

RacFit Foundation

On and off
the court.

The RacFit Foundation provides access to sports, wellness and educational opportunities that empower families and foster lifelong well-being - including the Scharbauer-Wikse ACE Outreach Program, connecting positive role models with children through the sport of tennis.
