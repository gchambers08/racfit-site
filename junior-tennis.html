Ages 5–18

Every kid
starts somewhere.

Red, Orange and Green Ball use smaller courts, lower nets and slower balls, so kids rally from day one instead of chasing. When they’re ready the ball speeds up, the court grows, and the pathway keeps going - through high school training and High Performance, all the way to national-level competition and college prep.

Every level is taught on our 2 indoor and 6 outdoor courts, and youth programming is guided by nationally recognized junior coach Michael MacVay.

Levels & Schedules

Find their level.

What each group works on, when it meets, and what they need to show before moving up.

Ages 5–6

Red Ball

Tue & Thu 3:45–4:30 PM
Sat 9:15–10:00 AM

The ideal starting point for our youngest athletes. Open to beginner (pre-rally) players, who will learn cooperation and exchange with a partner to simulate a fundamental rally, an understanding of ball bounce and the physics of the tennis environment, counting in games to build toward scorekeeping, and fundamental tennis movements and reactions.

Moving up: to the 7–9 class when they display greater coordination, ball tracking, cooperative partner work and maturity.

Register for Red Ball →
Ages 7–9

Red Ball

Tue & Thu 4:30–5:30 PM
Sat 10:00–11:00 AM

Open to beginners and players entering the rally-ready stage. Heavy partner-to-partner work building toward seasonal events like the Red Ball City Championships and the in-house tournament and pizza party.

Exit skills: maintain a peer-to-peer rally on a 36′ court with a red training ball, show fundamental reactions on both forehand and backhand sides, produce a controlled overhand serve, and manage a match and keep score.

Register for Red Ball →
Ages 7–11

Orange Ball

Mon & Wed 5:00–6:30 PM
Sat 11:00 AM–12:30 PM

For players ready for longer rallies, bigger courts and real match skills with age-appropriate equipment.

Exit skills: generate topspin and backspin, navigate back, mid and front court while modifying swing size to match reaction time, create a continental grip spin serve, and understand doubles as well as singles.

Register for Orange Ball →
Ages 8–12

Green Ball

Tue & Thu 5:30–7:00 PM
Sat 11:00 AM–12:30 PM

Ideal for players building full-court skills, refining technique and preparing for competitive match play.

Exit skills: set up two-shot patterns using height, width and depth changes, and finish points at the net via open-court volley or overhead.

Register for Green Ball →

High Performance

By Approval

Tue, Wed & Thu 5:30–7:30 PM

Our structured developmental system, designed to maximize technical skill, tactical awareness, physical conditioning and mental resilience. Integrates on-court drilling, points-based play, strength and conditioning, periodized fitness, recovery work, sports psychology and competition planning.

Entry: players are approved by the Director of High Performance and grouped by both skill level and social peer group. An active competitive calendar is expected.
