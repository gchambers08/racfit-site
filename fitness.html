The Schedule

Something on
every day.

A full weekly schedule across two dedicated fitness rooms - most classes unlimited for members on every plan. Baseline members can reserve 7 days out, Premium 10 days.

Bootcamp

Full-body, high-output training that mixes strength, conditioning and core work into one relentless hour.

HIIT

Short, hard intervals with real recovery built in - the fastest route to conditioning when time is tight.

Circuit-Cardio

Station-based work that keeps the heart rate up and the boredom down. Scalable for every level in the room.

Full BodyFit

Balanced strength and movement across every major pattern - the class to build a base on.

Tone & Core

Focused strength and midline work that carries straight over to the courts. Serve harder, move better, hurt less.

Cardio Dance

Great music, zero judgment, a serious sweat. The most fun you'll have burning that many calories.

Strength / Stretch

Load then lengthen. Build strength through range and finish with the mobility work most people skip.

Fitness Room

Free access during open times for members - train on your own schedule when a class doesn't fit.

Interactive Fitness

High-energy sessions on the Digital Sports wall for agility, speed and cognitive training. Game-based, seriously effective.
