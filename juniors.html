Summer Sports Camp

All day. All summer.
Ages 5–15.

Our All Sports Summer Camp combines tennis, pickleball, fitness, games and arts & crafts into an exciting, all-day experience led by experienced staff. Campers of all skill levels are welcome - no experience necessary - and each day blends movement, creativity, structure and fun.

Registration for next summer opens soon. Everything below is how camp runs. Dates and rates for the coming season will be confirmed when registration opens - let us know you are interested → and we will reach out first.

Each day includes

• Professionally led tennis and pickleball sessions
• Age-appropriate fitness games and movement challenges
• Digital Sports room play
• Fun group games and team-building
• Dedicated arts & crafts time
• Lunch included daily
• Supervised breaks and hydration time

Sessions & schedule

Weekly sessions through the summer

Full day - 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM
Half day - 8:30 AM to 12:00 PM (ages 5–6 half-day only)

Morning (7:30–8:30 AM) and afternoon (4:00–5:30 PM) extended care available in our ActiveCare room, with organized play, crafts and activities until pickup.

Digital games.
