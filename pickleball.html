Adult Pickleball

Fast game.
Faster friendships.

Pickleball at RacFit blends fast-paced play, skill development and a vibrant social atmosphere. From competitive matches and level-based clinics to beginner sessions, specialty workshops, tournaments and themed events, players of all abilities can improve their game, enjoy friendly competition and connect with a welcoming community.

Intro to Pickleball

A welcoming introduction to the fundamentals - rules, strokes, scoring and positioning - helping new players gain comfort, confidence and readiness for real gameplay.

View Classes →

Pickleball 101 / 102

Develop consistent strokes, smart positioning and basic strategy through guided drills and controlled play, building confidence for competitive rallies and open play.

View Classes →

Open Play

Rotating doubles, ladder play and social games designed to mix skill levels and keep the action fast-paced. Competitive but friendly - and free daily for members.

View Open Play →

Specialty Clinics

Targeted, fun sessions focusing on key areas of the game - third shot, kitchen play, stacking - to sharpen technique, boost strategy and elevate performance.

Covered Courts

Four courts under shade. Texas-proof.

A steel shade structure covers four of our eight regulation courts, so August afternoons and surprise showers don't cancel your game. The other four sit out in the open for when the weather cooperates.
