The Experience

Built for
connection.

RacFit brings together racquet sports, wellness and elevated hospitality to create a corporate event experience unlike anything else in the area. Designed for connection and energy, our indoor and outdoor courts, curated programming and vibrant social spaces set the stage for events that feel both active and refined.

Round it out with Courtside Cantina - our full-service bar, complemented by food truck partners that add a relaxed, social dining atmosphere your guests will love.

On the courts

Professionally hosted tennis and pickleball experiences for all skill levels. Indoor tennis and covered pickleball courts make it reliable, all-weather - no weather-contingency plan needed.

Wellness & challenges

Premium fitness classes, wellness activations and team challenges built around your group and your goals.

Digital Sports

Our MultiBall interactive gaming wall - multiplayer games and fitness-based activities that work for every fitness level in the room.

Eat & drink

Courtside Cantina for elevated social gatherings, plus curated food truck offerings for casual, high-energy dining. Outside catering also allowed.

Indoors and out

Expansive outdoor greenspace with lounge seating and activation areas, plus 22,000 sq ft of climate-controlled indoor space.

Family-friendly

On-site ActiveCare childcare means your team can bring the family without arranging anything else.
