What Happens Here

Play first.
Stay after.

RacFit socials mix good play with a good time, and most of them land at Courtside Cantina. Every membership tier includes invites to socials and member events, plus entry into the annual club championships.

Beer & wine nights

Social tennis and pickleball paired with a drink and a crowd. Come with a partner or get put in a rotation.

Themed gatherings

Seasonal and holiday events, mixers and one-off formats that keep the calendar from getting repetitive.

Community Art Series

Artist receptions and wine nights hosted in our community spaces - free and open to the community.

Tournaments

In-house tournaments across tennis and pickleball, plus the annual club championships that every member can enter.

Leagues

Structured league play across levels for both sports - a real season, a team around you, and something to chase.
