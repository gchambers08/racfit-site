Open Play provides daily opportunities to play, compete, have fun and hang out. For tennis, that's organized match play plus fun doubles games like Dingles, 105, Liveball and tiebreakers. For pickleball, it's rotating doubles, ladder play and social games designed to mix skill levels and keep the action fast-paced. Whether you're sharpening your game, meeting new partners or just enjoying friendly competition, there's always a place for you on the courts.

Our goal is to keep costs closer to a public facility than a country club. Clinics generally range from $15–$35+, depending on length, group size and whether they're indoors or outdoors. Members enjoy discounted rates, plus a variety of free or included classes, open play, social events and other exclusive benefits.
