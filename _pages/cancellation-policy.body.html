Cancellation
Policy

What we need from you when plans change - and what you can expect from us when we are the ones who have to cancel.

Effective June 28, 2025

The short version

To keep things fair for our coaches and participants, we require 24 hours’ notice for private lesson cancellations and notice by 8:00 PM the night before for clinics and classes. Late cancellations or no-shows will be charged. Memberships require 30 days’ notice to cancel. Full policy below.

Cancellations by Participants

Private Lessons

  • Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance of the scheduled lesson time.
  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice will be charged in full.
  • Rescheduling may be possible based on coach availability but is not guaranteed.

Clinics, Group Classes, and Fitness Programs

  • Cancellations must be made by 8:00 PM the night before the scheduled session.
  • Same-day cancellations or no-shows will result in forfeited sessions or full charge.
  • Repeated no-shows may result in restricted access to future sign-ups.

Camps, Events, and Multi-Day Programs

  • Cancellations made more than 7 days in advance will receive a full refund, minus any processing fee if applicable.
  • Cancellations within 7 days of the start date are non-refundable, unless a documented emergency is approved by management.

Membership Cancellations

RacFit memberships are month-to-month and may be cancelled at any time with 30 days’ written notice.

  • Notice must be submitted via email to info@goracfit.com or through your member portal.
  • Billing will continue for 30 days from the date notice is received.
  • Access to the facility and member benefits remains active during that 30-day period.
  • A membership hold/pause may be requested but may last no more than 3 months.

Cancellations by RacFit

If RacFit must cancel a program, class, or session due to weather, staffing, or facility issues:

  • We will make every effort to reschedule.
  • If rescheduling is not possible, a full refund or credit will be issued.

Additional Notes

  • Credit for missed sessions due to illness or emergencies may be granted on a case-by-case basis.
  • All cancellation requests must be submitted via phone, email, or through the RacFit member portal.

Thank you for your understanding and for supporting a respectful, professional experience for all RacFit members and staff.
