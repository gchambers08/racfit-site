The Idea

Where the club
hangs out.

Every good club needs somewhere to land after the last point. Courtside Cantina is ours - a proper full-service bar built into the middle of the club, with tables inside, seating out on the patio, and sightlines straight onto the courts.

Meet here before a clinic. Debrief a three-setter here after. It's where league nights end, where socials happen, and where a Tuesday evening on court turns into a Tuesday night.
