Why RacFit

A club worth
working at.

Every role here comes with a complimentary membership, discounts on RacFit programs, health and wellness benefit options for most, performance-based bonuses and support for professional development and certification.

Two ways in.

Both roles are based at our facility on Robert S. Light Blvd in Buda. Read the full description, then send a resume and cover letter.

Director of Pickleball

Lead and grow our pickleball offering across 8 dedicated courts - 4 of them covered. You’d own programming, tournaments, leagues and instruction, recruit and manage the coaching team, and build the community around it all.

Looking for: 3+ years in pickleball programming, coaching or facility management, a proven record of building programs and events, and PPR or IPTPA certification (preferred).

Full Job Description →

Customer Experience Associate

Be the first face members and guests see. Check-ins, questions, tours, the pro shop and the front desk - keeping the welcome warm and the operation running smoothly.

Looking for: strong communication skills, a friendly and professional manner, weekend availability, and a genuine interest in health and wellness. Front desk experience and racquet stringing are both a plus.
